The team built a system for each department, and then came the portal: a single entry point to the twelve in-house systems and five third-party platforms, with each department's policies and support channels. The systems stay independent; the portal is where everyone starts. I ran requirements gathering, delivery acceptance and the permission matrix that defines who can see and do what.
