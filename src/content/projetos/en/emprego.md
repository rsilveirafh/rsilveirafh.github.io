Everything starts from a profile with the facts of my career and where each number comes from: measured, estimated or no number. Screening only rates a job a strong match when every required skill has evidence in that profile, and a gap stays written down as a gap.

The agent searches, screens and prepares the application; I decide and I send it. Each application is logged with the exact résumé version, and interview prep starts from what the company actually read.

I adapted the method from meu-emprego-novo for Claude Code and for a résumé kept as code.
