I managed the project on Seteg’s side. The platform was funded by FINEP (Brazil’s innovation agency, under the Brasil Mais Produtivo programme) with SENAI. I ran the Scrum ceremonies with the developer, built the goals module and drove the final deliveries through to production. I also prepared the monthly progress reports, wrote the closing report and put together the technical package to register the software with INPI, Brazil’s IP office.

Alongside it, I delivered emissions and ESG goals dashboards in Power BI (DAX) and carbon inventories following the GHG Protocol.
