**Essential** is a single scrolling page: introduction, about, what I do, selected work and contact.

**Complete** has five sections (Home, About, Portfolio, Gallery and Contact), a PT/EN toggle, a portfolio filter and a contact form.

The sections adapt to the person. Researchers get Research, Publications and Fieldwork; people who serve clients get Services, Testimonials or a Blog. The site runs on the person's own domain, works on phones and has no monthly hosting fee.

Made to order: get in touch through the contact page.
