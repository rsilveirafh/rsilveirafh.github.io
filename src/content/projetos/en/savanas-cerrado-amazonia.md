In my plant ecology postdoc at UNEMAT, in Nova Xavantina (Mato Grosso), supervised by Eddie Lenza, I swapped reptiles for trees. The question was how soil explains the structure, diversity and seasonality of woody vegetation in the savannas of the Cerrado–Amazon transition.

I co-supervised Arthur Aires Veríssimo's master's thesis on the subject, which led to the paper published in *Austral Ecology* (2024).

I also taught: Introduction to R in the Ecology and Conservation graduate programme, and Field Ecology with the University of Brasília. I sat on master's committees and on seven faculty hiring committees.
