I coordinated the Innovation team, directly managing one developer with an individual development plan, and was the area’s point of contact with the board and the PMO.

I measured the gain from generative AI in three departments using logged hours: about 70% less time on the assessed tasks. I also wrote the company’s first AI usage policy.

I also wrote the team’s operating manual: how a requirement is written, how git is used, how the Kanban boards are kept and what the delivery cycle looks like, from request to handover. It includes a 5-day onboarding and a scope gate: no repository opens without an approved scope.

On funding, I wrote Annex I of a proposal for an ANEEL R&D call (budget, schedule, matching funds and indicators) and structured a FINEP proposal on generative AI.
